Last year, during the alien invasion, I was riding my Drahtesel along
 the Hauptstrasse when I suddenly saw a Porsche being driven by a gray 
 alien wearing a Dirndel. I was amazed by her driving skills, so I offered 
 her some Broetchen from a nearby Baeckerei, but she said she had just 
 eaten Apfelkuchen. I asked her if she had gotten it from the Baeckerei 
 down the street, but she replied that it was from the bakery next to the 
 Doener restaurant. She seemed interesting, so I invited her to the 
 Biergarten. We went there, had a few beers, and listened to Rammstein
